QUESTION <the one thing a person has to decide, in one line>
A. <the first option, and what taking it means>
B. <the second>

The shape a seat's question takes, and the only one `fleet ask` reads. What is
written here under no marker is this note, which teaches it and is never written
to an item.

`QUESTION` sits at column zero on the first line and carries the question
itself. Every line under it that opens with a capital letter, a period and a
space is an option; `fleet ask` refuses a note that names none, because a
question with no options is a conversation and a person answering one from a
phone has nothing to press.

The whole note is the gate's reason and the whole note is carried into the park,
so anything else written here — a paragraph of context, a link, what the seat
already tried — reaches the person who answers and the seat that resumes.

An option is what the ANSWER's letter names. A letter the options do not carry
is still answerable, with `--text`, because the person deciding may see a third
way the seat did not.
