The one shape a park writes, and the flight renders it whole. What is written
here under no marker is this note, which teaches it and is never written to an
item.

`PARKED` shares no prefix with a delivery's markers, a verdict's or a landing's,
so a reader anchoring on the last of those at column zero never reads a park as
one of them — and a park appended after any of the three ends that region rather
than extending it.

The four lines under the marker are what a person answering the gate needs and
what a later flight's takeoff reads to cut a seat's worktree: the reason is the
question, the branch and the commit are where the work got to, and the gate is
the store's own object the answer resolves. A reading nobody could take is
written `(none)` rather than left blank, because a blank line and an unread one
are the same bytes and not the same fact.

question: {question}

{options}
answer:   {answer}

The section a resumed seat reads above its item, appended after the order slot
of the brief the takeoff pins. It is written to no item: a park's own note is
the record, and this is that record rendered for the one seat that has to act
on it.

`RESUME` shares no prefix with a delivery's markers, a verdict's, a landing's or
a park's, so nothing that anchors on one of those reads a brief's section as a
note.

The commit and the branch are where the work got to and where this seat's
worktree is cut from, so the first thing it reads is also the first thing it
stands on. The question and the options are the park's, verbatim; the answer is
the letter a person chose and whatever they said beyond it.
