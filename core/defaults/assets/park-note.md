PARKED {item} — {reason}
branch:  {branch}
commit:  {commit}
hold:    {hold}

The one shape a park writes, and the flight renders it whole. What is written
here under no marker is this note, which teaches it and is never written to an
item.

`PARKED` shares no prefix with a delivery's markers, a verdict's or a landing's,
so a reader anchoring on the last of those at column zero never reads a park as
one of them — and a park appended after any of the three ends that region rather
than extending it.

The four lines under the marker are what a person clearing the hold needs and
what a later flight's takeoff reads to cut a seat's worktree: the reason is the
question, the branch and the commit are where the work got to, and the hold is
the store's own object the clearance clears. A reading nobody could take is
written `(none)` rather than left blank, because a blank line and an unread one
are the same bytes and not the same fact.
